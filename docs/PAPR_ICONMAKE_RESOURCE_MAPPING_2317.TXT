PAPR ICONMAKE PCX ↔ GEODE RESOURCE HEADER COMPARISON
Investigation 2317
Date: 2026-11-09

Inputs
------
- User-supplied PAPR_CGA.PCX
- User-supplied PAPR_HGC.PCX
- User-supplied PAPR_EGA.PCX
- GEOWORKS/ICONS/PAPR.000 from GEOWORKS.zip

PCX files were opened with Pillow and their dimensions, mode, and pixel
planes inspected. PAPR.000 was parsed with the repository's bounds-checked
GeoWorks 1.x GEODE reader. Resources R4-R6 were extracted without changes.

Observed PCX properties
-----------------------
PAPR_CGA.PCX: 48 x 14, mode 1 (one-bit monochrome)
PAPR_HGC.PCX: 48 x 30, mode 1 (one-bit monochrome)
PAPR_EGA.PCX: 48 x 30, mode P (paletted); PCX header specifies 4 bits per
pixel in one plane.

Observed provider payload headers
----------------------------------
Each R4-R6 is an LMem resource with a two-entry handle table. The large
second chunk starts at resource offset 0x2C. Its first 16 bytes are:

R4:
  61 30 00 0E 00 1A 65 00 30 00 0E 00 01 00 00 7F

R5:
  41 30 00 1E 00 1A D6 00 30 00 1E 00 01 00 00 7F

R6:
  46 30 00 1E 00 1A BD 02 30 00 1E 00 01 11 00 7F

A clear structural correspondence is visible:
- R4 contains the values 0x0030 and 0x000E at payload offsets +8/+10:
  48 x 14, matching the CGA PCX dimensions.
- R5 contains 0x0030 and 0x001E at +8/+10:
  48 x 30, matching the HGC PCX dimensions.
- R6 contains 0x0030 and 0x001E at +8/+10:
  48 x 30, matching the EGA PCX dimensions.

The following word at +12 differs:
- R4: 0x0001
- R5: 0x0001
- R6: 0x1101

Interpretation and confidence
-----------------------------
The matching width/height fields provide strong evidence for the mapping:
  R4 ↔ CGA (48x14)
  R5 ↔ HGC (48x30)
  R6 ↔ EGA (48x30)

This is a header-level match, not yet a complete pixel-data decode. The
difference 0x0001 versus 0x1101 is consistent with R6 using a different
pixel/moniker encoding from R4 and R5, but the exact meaning of 0x11 is not
yet proven from the 1.x structure definitions. Do not label it "4 bpp"
without that definition or a full decoder.

Integrity
---------
No source or original GEODE was modified. No new GEO was built. TOKEN.DB
was not changed. The PCX files were only read. This audit is restricted to
GeoWorks 1.x; no PC/GEOS 2.x or FreeGEOS definitions were used.
