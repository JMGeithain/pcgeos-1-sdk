Cross-provider confirmation
---------------------------
The GDOC extraction produces the same documented standard dimensions as
the PAPR extraction:
- CGA: 48 x 14, 1-bit monochrome
- HGC: 48 x 30, 1-bit monochrome
- EGA: 48 x 30, 4-bit paletted

The earlier static examination of GDOC.000 resource payload headers found:
- R4 begins 61 30 00 0E 00 1A ... and carries dimension-like words 48, 14
- R5 begins 41 30 00 1E 00 1A ... and carries dimension-like words 48, 30
- R6 begins 46 30 00 1E 00 1A ... and carries dimension-like words 48, 30

This independently supports the same resource mapping proposed from PAPR:
  R4 ↔ CGA
  R5 ↔ HGC
  R6 ↔ EGA

Confidence and limits
---------------------
The agreement of the independent GDOC PCX extraction dimensions with the
previously parsed R4-R6 dimension-like header fields substantially
strengthens the mapping. This is still a header/dimension correspondence,
not a complete pixel-for-pixel decode of the native GEODE resource payload.
The exact meaning of the 0x1101 field in R6 and transparency semantics
remain unresolved.

Note: the PAPR_EGA.PCX extraction used nine distinct palette indices.
GDOC_EGA.PCX uses six. This is a provider-specific image palette difference,
not a contradiction of the shared 4-bit EGA format.
