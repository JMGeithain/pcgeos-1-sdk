GEOWORKS 1.x ICON PROVIDER — LMem PAYLOAD ANALYSIS
Investigation 2312
Date: 2026-11-09

Scope
-----
Static analysis of resources R4, R5 and R6 in:
- GEOWORKS/ICONS/PAPR.000
- GEOWORKS/ICONS/GDOC.000
- GEOWORKS/ICONS/NBAT.000

The resources were extracted from the user's GEOWORKS.zip and parsed with
pcgeos1/geode.py. LMem parsing below is a structural interpretation of the
resource bytes. No runtime test, resource patch, GEO build, or TOKEN.DB
modification was performed.

LMem layout verified
--------------------
For each of R4, R5 and R6 in all three files:
- LMem header begins at resource offset 0.
- The handle count field at offset 0x0A is 2.
- Handle table begins at offset 0x20.
- Handle 0x20 points to chunk data at offset 0x26.
- Handle 0x22 points to chunk data at offset 0x2A.
- The chunk length word is stored immediately before each chunk's data.
- Chunk at handle 0x20 has length word 0x0004 and two payload bytes 00 02.
- Chunk at handle 0x22 contains the large provider-specific payload.

Thus these resources are not flat icon byte arrays: they are minimal LMem
heaps with a two-entry handle table and two chunks.

-------------------
The second chunk (handle 0x22) has these measured lengths and leading bytes:

     41 30 00 1E 00 1A BF 00 30 00 1E 00 01 00 00 01 ...

     61 30 00 0E 00 1A 52 00 30 00 0E 00 01 00 FB 00 ...

The length values above are the actual chunk payload lengths (the chunk
length word itself is excluded).

Repeated patterns and cautious interpretation
----------------------------------------------
1. R4 payloads begin with bytes 61 30 followed by 00 0E 00 1A.
2. R5 and R6 payloads begin with 41 30 or 46 30 followed by 00 1E 00 1A.
3. In little-endian interpretation, the pairs 00 0E and 00 1A correspond
   to 14 and 26; 00 1E and 00 1A correspond to 30 and 26.
4. These values are consistent with possible width/height fields, but this
   is not proven by the current evidence. The leading words 61 30, 41 30
   and 46 30 are also not assigned semantic names here.
5. Payloads differ among providers, including substantial differences in
   the data after the common prefixes.

No claim is made yet that R4 is a monochrome icon, R5 is a color icon, or
R6 is a document icon. The bytes may represent encoded bitmap/moniker data,
but a definitive VisMoniker decode requires a matching GeoWorks 1.x
structure definition or a controlled comparison with known 1.x moniker
resources.

Consequences for GEOTEST
------------------------
- The native provider pattern includes LMem resources, not only a raw PCX.
- A provider's payload resources can each be minimal two-handle LMem heaps.
- The 00 02 chunk at handle 0x20 is common to all nine inspected resources.
- The large chunk at handle 0x22 is the provider-specific payload.
- We should not copy resource R4/R5/R6 blindly into GEOTEST. First identify
  their moniker format and determine which resource is selected for which
  display context.

Next verification step
----------------------
Compare these payloads against independently known GeoWorks 1.x VisMoniker
and bitmap resources, ideally from the same installation. Decode the
resource's leading fields and any packed pixel data. Only after that should
we create native color and monochrome test-document icon resources.

Integrity / safety
------------------
Original PAPR.000, GDOC.000, NBAT.000 and GEOWORKS.zip were not modified.
No new GEO was built. TOKEN.DB was not touched.
No PC/GEOS 2.x or FreeGEOS definitions were used.
