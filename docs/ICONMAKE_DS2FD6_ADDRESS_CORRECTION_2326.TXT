ICONMAKE DS:2FD6 — ADDRESS LABEL CORRECTION TO INVESTIGATION 2325
Investigation 2326
Date: 2026-11-09

Correction
----------
Investigation 2325 listed some displacement-byte offsets as though they
were instruction-start offsets. The raw scan found the byte sequence D6 2F
at the following load-module offsets. These are the displacement field
positions, not instruction starts:

Displacement offset  Instruction start  File instruction start
0x18F9              0x18F6             0x1AF6
0x1914              0x1912             0x1B12
0x1BA5              0x1BA2             0x1DA2
0x1CE6              0x1CE4             0x1EE4
0x1CF2              0x1CF0             0x1EF0

The instruction bytes and decoded operations are:
- 0x18F6: 88 87 D6 2F — MOV byte [BX+2FD6],AL
- 0x1912: 88 9F D6 2F — MOV byte [BX+2FD6],BL
- 0x1BA2: 8A 87 D6 2F — MOV AL,byte [BX+2FD6]
- 0x1CE4: 8A 87 D6 2F — MOV AL,byte [BX+2FD6]
- 0x1CF0: 8A 87 D6 2F — MOV AL,byte [BX+2FD6]

The second store loop is therefore:
  1910  2B DB             SUB BX,BX
  1912  88 9F D6 2F       MOV [BX+2FD6],BL
  1916  43                INC BX
  1917  83 FB 10          CMP BX,10h
  191A  7C F6             JL 1912

This confirms a zero-fill of 16 bytes at DS:2FD6 through DS:2FE5.
The first store instruction starts at 0x18F6, not 0x18F9; 0x18F9 is
where its displacement begins.

This correction is an address-label clarification; it does not change
the conclusions that there are two actual stores and three actual reads.
The first store loop's semantics and the source of its AL values remain
unresolved.
